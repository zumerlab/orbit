Sides

A .side is a straight line between two neighbors on a ring. Put N sides on a ring and they close a regular polygon with N corners.

A side is 1px thick, in the text color. Its look is plain CSS: height for the thickness and background for the color. Its length comes from the number of sides on the ring, so it has no size classes.

Inside or around the ring

By default the corners of the polygon sit on the ring. With .outer-orbit on every side, the middle of each side touches the ring instead, and the polygon wraps around it.

Partial polygons

Sides follow the ring's --o-range and --o-from: three sides on range-180 are half a hexagon. To put a single side at a given angle, use .angle-N on it, as with satellites: angle-90 is the right edge.

A side can carry a .capsule for a label along its edge. See Capsules.
