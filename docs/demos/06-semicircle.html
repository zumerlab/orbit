Half gauge

A battery level as 20 cells on a half circle. Below 30% it turns amber, below 15% coral.

How it's built

  • The universe is 2:1 with .at-bottom, so the center sits on the bottom edge.
  • --o-force: 100cqw makes the reach the width instead of the shorter side.
  • 20 <o-arc> without value split range-180 from-270; lit cells get a class.
