Speedometer

A 270° dial. Ticks, numbers and colored zones live on separate rings that share range-270 from-225, so they always line up.

How it's built

  • fit-range puts the first and last tick on the ends of the range: 45 minor and 12 major .vectors.
  • Numbers are satellites on lane 8; zones are three stacked <o-arc value> on lane 11.
  • The needle is one .vector nine lanes long. Its ring turns with rotate, which transitions smoothly.
  • The readout is a satellite placed at the bottom with .angle-180.
