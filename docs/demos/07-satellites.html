Team map

People you work with, by how often you work together. Hover a face to see who it is.

How it's built

  • Three rings of satellites on lanes 5, 8 and 11; Orbit spreads each group evenly.
  • Each ring sets its own --o-size-ratio, so closer people are bigger.
  • from-N shifts a ring so faces on different rings don't line up.
  • The dashed paths are empty rings with a border.
