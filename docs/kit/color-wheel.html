Color wheel

<o-color-wheel> is a color picker: a hue ring around a saturation and brightness square, with optional swatches.

Drag around the ring for the hue, and inside the square for saturation (left to right) and brightness (bottom to top). The hue handle is the only satellite of an orbit-12 whose --o-from is the hue, so Orbit puts it on the ring; the ring and the square sit in center capsules. A gray keeps the hue you had, so turning the saturation back up brings it back.

Attributes

themedark, light or glass. Also inherited from an ok-theme-* class on a container. See Shared options.

Properties and events

NameWhat it does
valueThe color as hex. Setting it takes any CSS color and fires no event, like a native input.
rgb, hslThe color as rgb() and hsl() text.
colorNameA plain name, like “dark blue” or “light gray”.
form, labels, defaultValue, validityAs on a native input.
inputFires on every change you make. event.detail has value (hex), rgb, hsl and name.
changeFires once a drag ends with a different color, on each key press and on each swatch you pick.
 
picker.addEventListener('input', (e) => preview(e.detail.value))
picker.value = 'tomato'
picker.value // '#ff6347'

Accessibility

The picker is a role="group" with two sliders, each one tab stop. The hue slider is called “Hue” and reads like “210 degrees, blue”; arrows move it one degree, Shift and Page Up or Page Down ten, Home and End go to the ends, and it wraps around. The square is called “Saturation and brightness”: Left and Right change the saturation, Up and Down the brightness, one percent at a time or ten with Shift. It reads like “Saturation 80%, brightness 60%, dark blue, #1f4c99”.

Swatches are buttons named after their label or their color, with aria-pressed on the one that matches. They share one tab stop; arrows move between them and Enter or Space picks one.
