Time picker

<o-time-picker> picks a time by turning a clock: hours first, then minutes.

The dial is an Orbit universe. Hours and minutes are two rings of twelve satellites on orbit-10, and only one shows at a time. The hand is a .vector and the knob a satellite on the same ring; both are turned by one registered custom property, so switching from hours to minutes sweeps them round the dial instead of jumping. The fields above are ordinary elements, for reading and for the keyboard.

24 hours

With hours="24" the dial gets an inner ring for 00 and 13 to 23, like a phone's clock. Without the attribute, the picker follows the hour cycle of the page's language.

Accessibility

The picker is a role="group" whose name says the time. The hours and the minutes are each a role="spinbutton" and a tab stop, with aria-valuenow, aria-valuemin, aria-valuemax and aria-valuetext; AM and PM are toggle buttons with aria-pressed. The dial repeats what the fields say, so it is hidden from screen readers.

Up and Down change the focused field: hours round the day, minutes by minute-step round the hour, skipping anything outside min and max. Page Up and Page Down take bigger steps, digits type a value, Left and Right move between fields, and A or P switch the half of the day. Focusing a field turns the dial to it.
