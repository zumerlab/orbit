Fitness and health apps

Activity rings made the circle the way to show a daily goal: you see how close you are without reading a number. With Orbit, rings, gauges and watch faces are a handful of HTML elements, updated by setting a value.

Where it fits

  • Goal rings for steps, calories, exercise, water or sleep.
  • Live readouts during a workout, like heart rate from a Bluetooth sensor.
  • Summary screens with several radial figures.
  • Web and PWA versions of an app, without a chart library. Give each ring role="meter" or progressbar and its value, and screen readers announce it.

Where it does not

  • Trends over days, like weight or sleep history. Use a charting library.
  • Route maps and heatmaps.

Start from

  • Progress ringsThree goals with rounded ends.
  • Stacked progressThree goals around one center.
  • 180° gaugeA zone readout, like heart rate.
  • Smartwatch faceLive time with complications.

A daily summary could be three rings at the top, a heart-rate gauge under them and a row of small dials for water, sleep and steps. The Kit rings component does the first part.
