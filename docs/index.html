HTML on circles.

Orbit is a CSS layout system for things that grow around a center: gauges, dials, knobs, radial menus, donut charts, watch faces. You write plain HTML with a few classes. Orbit does the trigonometry.

A small solar system

Orbit borrows its words from the sky. It sounds like a gimmick for about a minute. After that it is the easiest way to remember what each piece does.

  • .bigbang

    The universe. Its box is the only size you set.

  • .gravity-spot

    The center. Its gravity reaches the edge of the universe.

  • .orbit-1 … .orbit-12

    Twelve lanes from the center out to that edge.

  • .satellite

    Travels a lane. Orbit spreads them around it and keeps your content upright.
