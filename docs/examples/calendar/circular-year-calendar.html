Circular year calendar

A year on one dial: seasons, months and the days of the month on three rings. Step through the days and the day ring re-spaces itself for 28, 30 or 31 days.

How it's built

  • The days are satellites on orbit-11. The script writes as many as the month has, and Orbit spaces them evenly, so the ring is always full. See satellites.
  • Months and seasons are <o-arc> elements without a value: sibling arcs share their ring equally. Text inside an arc is drawn along it, and flip keeps the lower half readable.
  • from-350 turns the season ring so winter starts on 21 December, ten days before January.
  • The thin band between the months and the days is an <o-progress max="365"> set to the day of the year.
