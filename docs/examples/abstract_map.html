Abstract orbital map

A star chart where every planet is a system of its own, with its own rings and moons. Each ring turns at its own speed with a plain CSS animation on rotate.

How it's built

  • Each planet is a satellite with its own .gravity-spot, so it gets its own twelve lanes for a core, a dashed ring and moons. See satellites.
  • Rings are drawn by their border. An empty ring is just a circle, so the chart's guides are rings with nothing on them; the ticks and meridians are vectors.
  • A ring with rotate turns everything on it. The .turn class animates it, and --time and --dir on each ring set the speed and the direction.
  • The label rides on orbit-16 of the planet's system, past its edge. That ring turns back as fast as the planet's ring, so the two cancel out and the word stays upright beside the planet.
