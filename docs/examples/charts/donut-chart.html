Donut chart

Arcs with a value follow each other around the ring, so a donut chart is just your data in order.

How it's built

  • Each room is an <o-arc value>. Values are shares of 100 (or of max), and each arc starts where the one before it ends.
  • One ring on lane 8, four lanes thick, leaves room in the middle for the total. --o-gap: 2 draws the thin line between segments.
  • The shares are satellites on the same lane, turned with angle-N to the middle of their arc: the running total before it plus half its own share, times 3.6.
  • Switching weeks only rewrites the values, a little at a time; Orbit restacks the arcs on every frame.
