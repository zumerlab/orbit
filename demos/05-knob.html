How it's built

  • The body is a satellite 15 lanes wide on orbit-0. A satellite is a universe too, so the .gravity-spot inside it places the indicator dot relative to the knob.
  • Turning the knob turns that inner ring with rotate.
  • Ticks and the progress band share range-270 from-225; ticks light up with a class.
